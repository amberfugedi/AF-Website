What’s missing from the story

The diagnosis, measured

  • The Approach is a deliverables list, not a decision. Scan-Optics fits seven deliverables into 33 words with five action verbs: defined, implemented, launched, ran, rebuilt. A reader learns what you did and never learns what you chose. Anyone can list HubSpot and ABM. The judgment is the part only you have.
  • There is no turn. Every case goes situation → activity → numbers. Nothing was ever at risk, nothing was ever difficult, and nothing could have gone another way. That is a résumé entry, not a story.
  • Only one case out of six reflects. PERQ closes with “What it changed about how I lead” and it is the most human paragraph on the page. The other five end on a number and stop.

N1 — Add the decision

One new beat between the situation and the result: the call you made, and what you traded for it. Tinted so the eye lands on it, because in a portfolio this is the most valuable paragraph in the case. Everything else stays as it is.
