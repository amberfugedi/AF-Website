Exterior cleaning for Wethersfield homes

Wethersfield has houses of every age, from the colonials and capes in Old Wethersfield's historic district to newer homes with vinyl siding. Older homes often have painted wood clapboard, which needs a gentle low-pressure wash. High pressure can strip paint and push water into old wood. Mature trees shade many Wethersfield streets, and shaded walls and roofs are where algae and mildew take hold first.
