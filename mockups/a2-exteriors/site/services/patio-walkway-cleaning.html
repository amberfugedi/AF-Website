Patio and walkway cleaning

Pavers, brick, and stone go green and slick in the shade, and moss fills the joints. We clean them with pressure matched to the material, so you get the color back without chipped edges or etched stone.

Get a Free Estimate Call or text 860-430-4568
Can pavers be pressure washed?

Yes, with care. Pavers, brick, and most natural stone clean up well with pressure. Too much pressure, though, blasts sand out of the joints, chips paver edges, and etches softer stone like bluestone. The fix is lower pressure, the right nozzle, and a wand that keeps moving.

What we clean

  • Concrete pavers and brick patios
  • Bluestone, flagstone, and other natural stone
  • Stamped and plain concrete patios
  • Front walks, garden paths, and stone steps

Problems it fixes

  • Slippery green algae on walks and steps that stay shaded. Removing it makes the surface safer underfoot.
  • Moss and growth in joints between pavers and stones
  • Grill grease, food, and furniture marks on patios
  • General dirt that dulls the color of brick and stone

White haze on pavers, called efflorescence, comes from salts inside the material. Washing alone may not remove it. Whether A2 treats efflorescence

What to expect

  • Before: move furniture, grills, and planters off the patio, or ask us to move them.
  • Joint sand: cleaning can loosen some sand between pavers. Whether A2 re-sands joints after cleaning
  • After: the surface dries in a few hours. Weeds rooted deep in joints can grow back. Cleaning removes surface growth, not roots.

When to book

Book in spring to get the patio ready for the season, or any time a shaded walk turns slick. If you plan to seal pavers, have them cleaned first.

Patio and Walkway Cleaning questions

Will cleaning wash out the sand between my pavers?

Some sand can loosen, especially in older joints. Using lower pressure and angling the spray away from the joints keeps loss to a minimum. Re-sanding availability

Does patio cleaning kill weeds?

It removes moss and surface growth from joints. Weeds with deep roots can return, since washing does not reach the root.

Why is my walkway slippery?

A thin film of algae grows on stone and concrete that stays shaded and damp. It turns slick when wet. Cleaning removes the film and makes the surface easier to walk on.
