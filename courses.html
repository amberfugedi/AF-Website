One channel is not a foundation.

Built for the part after the advice.

Most business education gives you more ideas. The harder part is knowing which ideas matter, what to prioritize and how to apply them to the business you actually have.

These courses are designed to help you think through the work, not just watch someone explain it. You will leave with clearer decisions, practical tools and something you can use.

The first course

Build Your Marketing Foundation

A practical course for service-based businesses that have paying clients but need a clearer, more dependable approach to marketing.

Instead of adding more tactics, you will evaluate the foundation underneath your marketing, identify the gaps holding it back and decide what deserves your attention next.

  • 8 modules
  • About 3 hours
  • Self-paced
  • Includes the Marketing Foundation Map

View the course See what is included

A tactic can work and still not be the thing your business needs next.

Marketing works better when the foundation works together.

The course is organized around three layers and seven foundations that help a business get found, get chosen and be remembered.

Get Found

Help the right people discover the business.

Get Chosen

Give people enough clarity and confidence to take the next step.

Be Remembered

Build the trust, familiarity and relationships that create future demand.

The seven foundations run through all three

  1. Positioning
  2. Getting Found
  3. Earned Proof
  4. Referrals
  5. Awareness
  6. Owned Audience
  7. Authority

The course treats these as one system. Strengthening one usually changes what the next one needs.

You will not leave with a longer marketing list.

You will leave knowing:

  1. What is already working and should be protected
  2. Where your marketing foundation is weakest
  3. Which opportunities deserve attention now
  4. Which tactics are distractions
  5. How customers currently find, choose and remember you
  6. What to improve next based on your business, capacity and goals

Designed for businesses that are already in motion.

This course is for service-based business owners, small teams and marketers who already have paying clients but feel like their marketing is inconsistent, reactive or overly dependent on referrals.

This course may be a good fit if

  • You know marketing matters but are unsure where to focus
  • You have tried several tactics without a clear system
  • Your business relies heavily on referrals
  • You want a stronger foundation before increasing your marketing spend
  • You need practical direction, not more inspiration

It may not be the right fit if

  • You have not yet defined what your business sells
  • You are looking for a guaranteed quick-growth formula
  • You want a course focused only on social media, paid ads or a single channel
  • You want someone else to make every decision for you

Made to be used, not just completed.

Each module combines clear instruction, a practical business example and guided work inside the Marketing Foundation Map. You can move through the course in order or return to individual sections as your business changes.

  1. Clear lessons

    Focused teaching without unnecessary filler.

  2. Real examples

    Practical scenarios that make the ideas easier to apply.

  3. Guided decisions

    Prompts that help you evaluate your own business.

  4. A working tool

    A completed Marketing Foundation Map you can continue using after the course.

Taught by someone who still does the work.
