Marketing Leadership & Growth

Growing a marketing function alongside the business

I joined PERQ to lead demand generation, took ownership of the whole marketing function, and shaped the department around where the business was going next.

How it progressed

I joined in December 2023 as Director of Demand Generation, moved to Director of Growth Marketing and Enablement, and was promoted to VP in January 2026. At the end of 2024 the whole marketing function came under my ownership, and I shaped the department around where the business was going next.

What I own

The function end to end, from brand and positioning through demand generation to marketing operations, with sales enablement inside a wider remit. I create the roles the business needs and develop the people in them, direct where the investment goes, and build the internal tooling that makes performance easier to read. I serve on the Executive Leadership Team, contributing to corporate strategy, annual business planning and board-level discussions, and the results below came from the whole team and the wider business working toward the same targets.

What it changed about how I lead

The hardest part was not the marketing. It was learning to start from what the business needed next, build the team around that, and make the investment decisions that followed. Most of my attention now goes to the conditions around the work: developing the people in the roles, and making investment calls someone can still follow a year later. I’ve learned that effective leadership is about creating the conditions for other people to do their best work.
