Card front design

Keep the fronts visually simple.

Each card should include:

* a small number
* the main card title
* the supporting line
* one restrained interaction cue

Do not use large plus icons.

A small arrow, “Open,” “See how I help,” or a subtle corner treatment is enough.

Use the site’s existing palette:

* warm cream
* charcoal
* coral
* butter
* soft pink
* lavender

The four cards can use restrained tonal variation, but do not make them look like four unrelated pastel tiles.

Card back design

Keep the back concise and readable.

Use sans serif for all card content.

Use serif only for the main section heading.

The back should not feel like a dense wall of copy or a second page hero.

Use clear spacing between:

* What may be happening
* How I help
* Best place to start
* This may look like
* CTA

Do not place every subsection in a separate mini-card.

Visual direction

The section should feel:

* warm
* practical
* approachable
* creative
* easy to understand
* clearly interactive
* more human than corporate

Avoid:

* SaaS feature-card styling
* oversized serif text
* heavy shadows
* rounded app-style tiles
* dashboards
* icons
* generic checkmarks
* scrapbook styling
* strong 3D animation
* hover-only behavior

Accessibility and behavior

* Use semantic buttons or accessible card controls.
* Support mouse, touch, and keyboard input.
* Include clear focus states.
* Only one card may be open at a time.
* Include an obvious way to return to the card front.
* Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
* Do not cause layout shifts when cards open.
* Ensure CTAs remain usable inside the open card.

Responsive behavior

On desktop:

* use a two-by-two grid
* keep equal card heights
* allow enough space for the longer third card title
* keep the card fronts easy to scan

On mobile:

* stack one card per row
* keep the full card title readable
* allow the card back to expand vertically when opened
* avoid horizontal scrolling
* keep tap targets large
* ensure the CTA appears without excessive internal scrolling

Before finishing, test all four cards on desktop, tablet, and mobile and confirm that the content, CTAs, keyboard behavior, and reduced-motion behavior work correctly.
