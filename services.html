Workflow & AI Guidance

AI can be useful, but only when it supports a real business need. I review your goals, processes, and day-to-day work to identify where AI, a better tool, or a clearer workflow could improve the way the work gets done. The goal is to make a thoughtful decision about what is worth changing.

  • Review goals, workflows, and current challenges
  • Identify practical AI opportunities tied to business goals
  • Find unnecessary friction and repetitive work
  • Improve the process before adding more tools
  • Create a realistic testing or implementation plan
  • Recommend automation only when it is the right solution
