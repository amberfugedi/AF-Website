Case study: design options

Same Scan-Optics content in every option, same tokens, same stylesheet. The diagnosis each one is answering: the case has no focal point. The company name and the metric figures are both 30px, so nothing leads, and the strongest content sits bottom-left in a row that is half empty.

Current

Metadata, title, scope, then Challenge / Approach / Impact. The figures arrive last, in the narrowest position on the page.

Document Technology · Director of Marketing

Scan-Optics

Demand generation, CRM, ABM, paid media, and events.

Challenge

A decades-old document technology company repositioning toward intelligent automation, with an established marketing function and an opportunity to bring it in line with the new direction.

Approach

Rebuilt marketing around the new positioning: defined the TAM and ICPs, implemented HubSpot CRM and automation, launched ABM, paid media, event, and outbound programs, and ran partner campaigns with Dropbox, UKG, and Xerox.

Impact

  • 115%more opportunity creation2021–2023
  • $2.8Mpipeline, up from $1.3M2021–2023
  • 47%more inbound leads2021–2023

Option A — Impact leads

The result comes first, as a full-width band under the title, then the explanation. Figures step up to 44px so they outrank the company name instead of tying with it. Nothing else changes.

Drops Challenge / Approach / Impact entirely. A serif lede carries the situation, two numbered movements carry the work, and the outcomes close as a single line. Least like a template, most like something she wrote.
