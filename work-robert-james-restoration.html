Consulting & Client Work

Building a presence for a business that ran on referrals

Restoration work arrives by word of mouth. Robert James had the reputation and none of the things a reputation needs to point at, so we built the whole presence from the ground up.

Nine years on, the business still runs its marketing on that foundation.

Client
Robert James Restoration
Industry
Restoration services
Role
Consultant, and still the marketing partner
Focus
Brand identity, website, business email, social media, review management, AI systems consulting

The engagement

A restoration business doing good work and ready to build a real presence around it. There was no brand, no website and nothing for a referral to look up afterwards, which in a trade where people check before they call is a gap between the reputation and the business it could bring in.

What I built

The brand itself, from the ground up, and the website it lives on. Business email accounts, so correspondence came from the company rather than from a personal address. The social media presence and the content behind it. Review management, which in restoration is the closest thing there is to advertising.

The work did not stop at launch. Nine years on I am still the marketing partner, which lately has included consulting on where AI genuinely supports the business and where it does not.

See the rest of the website

What changed

The business was, and still largely is, referral-driven. What the brand and the website added was a second way in. With a presence to be found and reviews actively managed, enquiries started arriving from search and from Google reviews on top of the referrals, from people who had never met anyone who had used them.

That is the quiet value of this kind of groundwork for a local service business. It does not replace word of mouth. It gives word of mouth somewhere to land, and it keeps working while nobody is tending it.
