Methods and results

What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high water pressure to remove dirt from hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to clean surfaces that high pressure can damage, like siding and roofs. Most homes need both, on different surfaces.

Can pressure washing damage siding?

Yes. High pressure can crack vinyl, force water behind siding and into wall cavities, strip paint, and splinter wood. That is why siding gets a low-pressure wash with a cleaning solution, and high pressure stays on concrete.

Can a roof be cleaned without damaging shingles?

Yes, with low pressure and a cleaning solution instead of a pressure washer. High pressure strips the protective granules from asphalt shingles. A soft wash kills the algae and moss without blasting the surface.

What causes dark stains on roofs?

Most dark streaks on asphalt roofs come from algae, usually Gloeocapsa magma, which grows on shaded, damp slopes and feeds on limestone filler in the shingles. Read more in what causes black streaks on your roof.

How do you remove buildup from concrete?

Concrete is porous, so dirt, mildew, and tire rubber settle into the surface instead of sitting on top. A rotary surface cleaner run at high pressure lifts most of it in even passes, and a pre-treatment helps with mildew and organic stains. Old oil and rust stains often lighten without disappearing completely.

Will every stain come out?

No. Algae, mildew, and dirt usually clean up well. Rust, oil, paint, and the chalky film on old vinyl or aluminum may lighten without disappearing. We point those out before we start so you know what to expect.

Scheduling and preparation

What should homeowners do before an exterior cleaning appointment?

Close windows and doors, bring pets inside, and move cars, patio furniture, toys, and anything fragile away from the areas we are cleaning. Unlock gates. Make sure an outdoor faucet works, since we connect to it.

How long does an exterior cleaning appointment take?

A house wash on a typical single-family home takes two to four hours. A driveway or patio often takes one to three hours. Roofs and commercial jobs vary more, and we will give you a time window with your quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually. If we can reach a working outdoor faucet and get through any gates, you can be at work.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain does not affect most cleaning. In heavy rain, wind, or freezing temperatures we reschedule and let you know as early as we can.

Is it safe for my plants and pets?

We wet plants before and after cleaning and rinse anything the runoff touches. Keep pets inside until the work is done and surfaces have been rinsed. Confirm plant care steps

How often should a house be washed?

Most homes need a wash every one to two years. Houses under trees, near wetlands, or with walls that stay in shade often need it every year.

When should I consider professional cleaning?

Call a professional when you see algae or mildew spreading, when a roof shows black streaks or moss, when a walkway turns slippery, or before you sell. Professional cleaning also makes sense on roofs and second stories, where ladder work and the wrong pressure carry real risk.
