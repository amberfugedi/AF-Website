Ways we can work together.

Sometimes you need someone to step in and lead. Sometimes there’s one problem that needs untangling. Sometimes the work itself has gotten harder than it needs to be. And sometimes you just need an experienced person to think it through with.

Those are different kinds of help, so I work a few different ways.

  • Fractional leadership

    When the business needs senior marketing leadership, but not another full-time hire.

    I step into the function, help set direction, work with the team, improve how decisions get made, and stay close enough to the work to know what’s actually happening.

    Explore fractional leadership

  • Consulting

    When there’s a specific marketing problem that needs focused attention.

    We work out what’s actually causing it, decide what needs to change, and build the right answer around it.

    Explore consulting

  • Workflow and AI

    When the way the work happens is creating more work.

    I help teams simplify the process first, then build practical AI tools or automations where they genuinely help, and teach people how to use them well.

    Explore workflow and AI

  • Mentorship and advisory

    When you don’t need someone to take over. You need another experienced person in the room.

    Bring the decision, the work, the leadership challenge, or the thing you keep circling. We’ll pressure-test it together and figure out the next move.

    Explore mentorship and advisory
