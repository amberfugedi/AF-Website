Workflow & AI Guidance

AI can be useful, but only when it supports a real business need. I review your goals, processes, and day-to-day work to identify where AI, a better tool, or a clearer workflow could improve the way the work gets done. The goal is to make a thoughtful decision about what is worth changing.

  • Review goals, workflows, and current challenges
  • Identify practical AI opportunities tied to business goals
  • Find unnecessary friction and repetitive work
  • Improve the process before adding more tools
  • Create a realistic testing or implementation plan
  • Recommend automation only when it is the right solution

Fractional marketing leadership

Senior marketing leadership on a retained basis, for businesses that need a marketing leader’s judgment without hiring one full time. I work inside the business rather than alongside it: setting the direction, running the plan, and staying close enough to the work that it keeps moving.

  • Direction and planning owned end to end, not handed over as a deck
  • Campaigns, demand generation, and paid media run against the plan
  • Website, content, and member or customer communications kept current
  • Reporting and systems the team can run without me
  • A steady presence rather than a one-off project
