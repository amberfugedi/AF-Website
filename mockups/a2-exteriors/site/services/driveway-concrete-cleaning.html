Driveway and concrete cleaning

Concrete takes a beating from road salt, leaf stains, tire marks, and mildew in the shady spots. Pressure cleaning brings back the original color in even passes, without the stripes a rental washer leaves behind.

How do you remove buildup from concrete?

Concrete is porous, so dirt, mildew, and tire rubber settle into the surface instead of sitting on top. A rotary surface cleaner run at high pressure lifts most of it in even passes, and a pre-treatment helps with mildew and organic stains. Old oil and rust stains often lighten without disappearing completely.

What we clean

  • Driveways and garage aprons
  • Sidewalks, front walks, and steps
  • Curbs and concrete edging
  • Pool decks, garage floors, other flatwork

Problems it fixes

  • Black mildew and green algae in shaded sections and along lawn edges
  • Leaf and tannin stains from fall, the brown outlines oak and maple leaves leave behind
  • Tire marks where cars turn and park
  • Winter grime from road salt and sand
  • Oil and rust stains, which usually lighten but may not lift completely

How we clean it

We use a rotary surface cleaner for open areas. It spins two jets under a hood a few inches above the concrete, so the whole surface gets the same pressure. That avoids the light and dark stripes, sometimes called zebra striping, that a handheld wand leaves. Edges, steps, and corners get detailed by hand. Pre-treatment and hot water details

What to expect

  • Before: move cars, bins, and planters off the concrete.
  • During: the surface stays wet and slippery while we work.
  • After: you can walk and drive on it once it dries, usually within a few hours on a warm day.

When to book

Spring clears off winter salt and sand. Late fall, after the leaves come down, lifts tannin stains before they set over winter. If you plan to seal your driveway, clean it first so the sealer bonds to concrete, not dirt.

Driveway and Concrete Cleaning questions

Will pressure washing get oil stains out of my driveway?

It depends on how old the stain is and how deep it soaked in. Fresh stains often come out. Older stains usually get lighter. We will tell you what to expect when we see it.

Can pressure washing damage concrete?

It can if someone uses too narrow a tip or holds it too close. That etches the surface and leaves stripes. A surface cleaner keeps the nozzle height and pressure even across the whole slab.

How soon can I drive on the driveway?

Once it dries. On a warm, sunny day that is usually a few hours.
