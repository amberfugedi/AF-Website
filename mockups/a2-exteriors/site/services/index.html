Soft wash or pressure wash?

Different surfaces need different cleaning. Getting this wrong is how siding cracks and shingles lose their granules.

Read the full comparison

Soft wash

Low pressure plus a cleaning solution. Used on siding, roofs, painted surfaces, and stucco.

Pressure wash

High pressure, often with a surface cleaner. Used on concrete, and with care on pavers, brick, and stone.
