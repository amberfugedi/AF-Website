Roof washing without high pressure

Those black streaks running down your roof are algae, and they spread. We clean roofs with a low-pressure treatment, the approach shingle makers recommend, so the granules stay on the shingles.

What causes black streaks on a roof?

Most dark streaks on Connecticut asphalt roofs come from algae, usually a type called Gloeocapsa magma. It feeds on the limestone filler in shingles and grows on slopes that stay shaded and damp. Algae itself is mostly cosmetic. Moss and lichen are the bigger concern because they hold moisture against the shingles and can lift their edges over time.

Why roofs get a soft wash

Asphalt shingles are covered in mineral granules that protect them from sun and weather. A pressure washer blasts those granules off, which shortens the life of the roof and can void a shingle warranty. The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association advises against pressure washing asphalt roofs for this reason.

A soft wash uses low pressure and a cleaning solution that kills the algae and moss where they grow. The streaks lift off with a rinse or wash away over the following weeks of rain.

What a roof wash covers

  • Black streaks and algae staining on shingles
  • Moss and lichen treatment
  • Rinsing gutters and downspouts after the roof is clean

Roof types: asphalt shingle roofs. Metal, cedar, and slate roofs on request.

What to expect

  • Results on the day: algae streaks usually lighten right away. Thick moss dies on contact but can take a few weeks of rain to let go of the roof.
  • Your yard: we wet plants under the drip line before and after, and rinse anything the runoff touches.
  • Access: how A2 works on roofs: ladder, roof access, steep-pitch limits.
  • Time: typical duration.

When to book

Book when you first notice streaks on the shaded side of the roof. Algae spreads across a slope, and moss is easier to treat before it thickens. A clean roof also helps when you are selling, since buyers and inspectors see the roof in every listing photo.

Keeping it clean longer

Algae comes back. How fast depends on shade and moisture. Trimming back overhanging branches lets the roof dry out sooner, and zinc or copper strips near the ridge slow regrowth as rain carries traces of metal down the slope.

Roof Washing questions

Can a roof be cleaned without damaging the shingles?

Yes, with low pressure and a cleaning solution instead of a pressure washer. High pressure strips the protective granules from asphalt shingles. A soft wash kills the algae and moss without blasting the surface.

Will the black streaks come back?

Eventually. Algae spores travel on the wind, so regrowth depends on how much shade and moisture your roof gets. Many roofs stay clear for a few years after a cleaning.

Do you remove moss?

We treat moss so it dies and releases from the shingles. Thin moss rinses off. Thick patches can take a few weeks of rain to clear after treatment. Whether A2 hand-brushes heavy moss
