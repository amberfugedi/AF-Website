Who it’s for

The business grew through referrals, reputation, relationships, or the owner’s own effort. Marketing was never designed as a system. It works, until it needs to work more reliably.

  • You have paying clients, and marketing has never been built on purpose.
  • You are doing the marketing yourself, or alongside someone who already wears several hats.
  • You want to know what matters, what to build first, and what can wait.

Marketing gets easier when the foundation is intentional.

Diagnosis before tactics.

Most marketing advice hands you tactics before it knows anything about your business. Post more. Run ads. Start a newsletter. You end up doing a little of everything and trusting none of it.

This course does the opposite. It works out what your business actually needs, and in what order, before it asks you to build anything.

Every module is a concept module, not a tactics module. The goal is not to send you off to build a website by Friday. It is to give you a working answer to the strategic question underneath the tactic, so the tactics you do choose are the right ones.

Seven foundations, across three layers.

The first is positioning, the answer everything else depends on. The other six are the parts of a marketing system, grouped by how a business is encountered, evaluated, and remembered.

Eight modules in total: positioning, a foundation audit that diagnoses where you stand, then one module for each of the six remaining foundations. Five real businesses run through every module — a therapist, a pressure washer, a wedding photographer, a chiropractor and a stylist — so you see the same framework produce genuinely different answers.

The Marketing Foundation Map

Not a worksheet. An interactive tool, built into the course, that reads your answers and builds a plan for your business rather than a generic one.

The same course produces a different plan for a therapist than for a pressure washer. The tool is what makes that true.

  1. You answer a short diagnostic, and it rates each of your seven foundations: high, medium or low priority for how your business actually works.
  2. As you move through the modules, your positioning, your gaps and your decisions save into one place automatically.
  3. By the end it has assembled your Foundation Plan, sorted into what to build first, next and later. Nothing to open, nothing to paste, nothing to keep track of.

A priority order, not a to-do list.

The course ends by assembling everything into your Foundation Plan: your seven foundations, sorted into what to build first, what comes next and what can wait. Specific to how your business actually works.

  1. First

    The foundation with the most to gain, the one worth your attention now.

  2. Next

    Real, but it waits until the first is doing its job.

  3. Later

    Solid enough for now. Named, so you can stop worrying about it.

Who’s teaching it

For more than ten years I have helped small businesses who are overwhelmed by marketing and do not know where to start. The pattern is almost always the same. Not a lack of effort. A lack of a foundation to build on.

I built this course because most marketing advice skips the part that actually matters: the thinking underneath the tactics. It is what I come back to every time, made into something you can work through in an afternoon and keep for good.
