Mentorship and advisory

Sometimes you just need another marketer in the room.

Marketing decisions are often made by whoever happens to be closest to them. That works until the decision matters and there’s nobody around who has made it before.

This is time with someone who has. Bring the campaign that isn’t working, the plan you’re unsure about, the team situation you can’t quite name, or the decision you keep circling.

Bring me something Read what people say

Marketing roles can be surprisingly solitary.

Especially in the situations where a second opinion would be worth the most.

  • You’re the only marketer, or the most senior one.
  • You’ve moved into leadership and are learning it in public.
  • Your manager isn’t a marketer and can’t pressure-test the call.
  • You’re responsible for work you haven’t done before.
  • You want to grow without copying somebody else’s career.
  • You need to explain marketing to executives who see it as a cost.
  • You own the business and are making marketing decisions without another senior marketer nearby.

What we can work through

Bring whatever is actually on your mind. It usually falls into one of these.

  • Leadership

    Running a team, and the decisions that come with it.

    • Managing a team
    • Prioritization
    • Delegation
    • Talking to executives

  • Strategy

    Thinking the plan through before the budget is committed to it.

    • Marketing plans
    • Demand generation
    • Positioning
    • Budget decisions

  • The actual work

    Real campaigns, real drafts, real feedback on what you’re about to ship.

    • Messaging
    • Campaign feedback
    • Websites
    • Presentations

  • Career

    The bigger moves, and whether now is the moment to make one.

    • Moving into leadership
    • Role changes
    • Interview decisions
    • What comes next

You don’t need to turn your problem into a lesson before we can talk about it.

How it works

Some people book one session about one decision. Others keep a standing slot while they settle into a new role. Both are fine, and you don’t have to know which you want before getting in touch.

  • Bring the real problem

    The campaign, the decision, the team situation. It doesn’t need to be tidied up before it is worth talking about.

  • Talk it through properly

    Not a status update. We stay with it until the actual shape of the thing is clear.

  • Pressure-test the options

    I’ll push on the thinking rather than agree with it. That is the part you came for.

  • Leave with a next move

    A clearer decision, or the step that comes next. Something you can act on this week.

This is probably a fit if…

  • You’re growing into a marketing leadership role.
  • You’re the only marketer, or the most senior one on your team.
  • You want feedback from someone who has actually led marketing.
  • You want a sounding board without commissioning a whole project.
  • You run a business and want an experienced marketing opinion without a full consulting engagement.
  • You’d rather be challenged than agreed with.
  • You’re weighing a career or leadership decision and want a view from someone who has made similar ones.
What this isn’t

Not therapy, not life coaching, and not a résumé service. I’m also not doing the work for you: if the business needs someone hands-on inside it, that’s consulting or fractional leadership instead.
