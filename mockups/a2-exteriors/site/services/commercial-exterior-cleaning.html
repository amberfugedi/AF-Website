Commercial exterior cleaning

Customers see your entrance before they see anything inside. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, and building exteriors so your property looks cared for from the parking lot.

What does commercial exterior cleaning include?

Commercial exterior cleaning covers the outside surfaces customers and tenants see: building walls, storefronts and entryways, sidewalks, and walkways. The method changes by surface. Concrete gets pressure cleaning, and painted or coated walls get a low-pressure wash. Scope gets set on a walkthrough of your property.

What we clean

  • Storefronts and entry areas
  • Sidewalks, walkways, and building approaches
  • Building walls and facades
  • Dumpster enclosures, service alleys, and parking areas
  • Drive-throughs

Properties we work with

Retail storefronts, offices, restaurants, and multi-unit residential properties. If you manage several buildings, we can quote them together.

How we schedule

  • Early morning or after-hours work so customers and tenants aren’t disrupted
  • One-time cleanings or a recurring schedule
  • Certificate of insurance available on request

Wash water

Connecticut has rules about what can go into storm drains, and they apply to commercial washing. How A2 handles runoff on commercial sites
