Who it’s for

The business grew through referrals, reputation, relationships, or the owner’s own effort. Marketing was never designed as a system. It works, until it needs to work more reliably.

  • You have paying clients, but marketing has never been built on purpose.
  • You are doing the marketing yourself, or alongside someone who already wears several hats.
  • You have tried different tactics, but you are not confident they are the right priorities.
  • You want to know what matters, what to build first, and what can wait.

This is not for someone looking for a list of trending tactics or a shortcut to immediate growth. It is for a business ready to make more deliberate marketing decisions.

Marketing gets easier when the foundation is intentional.

Diagnosis before tactics.

Most marketing advice hands you tactics before it knows anything about your business. Post more. Run ads. Start a newsletter. You end up doing a little of everything and trusting none of it.

This course starts with diagnosis. It works out what your business needs, and in what order, before it asks you to build anything.

Every module is a concept module, not a step-by-step platform tutorial. The point is a working answer to the strategic question underneath the tactic, so the tactics you choose afterwards are the right ones.

Leave with a plan that tells you what comes first.

Questions

When does it open?

It is still in production and does not have a public date yet. Join the list above and you will get one note when it is ready.

Who is this course for?

Build Your Marketing Foundation is designed for established service businesses with paying clients. It is especially useful for owners or small teams whose marketing has grown through referrals, reputation, and individual effort rather than an intentional system.

Is this useful if I am already doing some marketing?

Yes. The course is not based on starting from zero. The Foundation Audit helps you assess what is already working, where gaps exist, and what deserves attention next.

How long does the course take?

The course contains about three hours of teaching across eight modules. You should also allow time to pause, think, and complete the work for your own business. The goal is not to rush through it in one sitting.

Does this teach me how to use specific marketing platforms?

No. This is a strategy and decision-making course, not a collection of platform tutorials. It helps you determine which parts of marketing matter for your business so you can make better tactical decisions afterward.

What will I have when I finish?

You will have your positioning, foundation priorities, identified gaps, key decisions, and a completed Foundation Plan organized into what to build first, next, and later.

Do I need separate software for the Foundation Map?

No. The Foundation Map is built into the course. Your answers and decisions save into one place as you work through the modules.

How long will I have access?

The course is yours to keep, so you can return to the lessons and your Foundation Map as your business evolves.
