Common questions

What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high water pressure to remove dirt from hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to clean surfaces that high pressure can damage, like siding and roofs. Most homes need both, on different surfaces.

How much does exterior cleaning cost?

Price depends on the size of the house or surface, how much buildup there is, and access. We give free estimates, and photos texted to 860-430-4568 help us quote faster.

Do you clean commercial properties?

Yes. We clean storefronts, entryways, sidewalks, and building exteriors. See commercial exterior cleaning.
