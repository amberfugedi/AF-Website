Build your marketing foundation.

A practical course for service-based businesses that have paying clients but need a clearer, more dependable approach to marketing.

Build your
marketing foundation

  • get found
  • get chosen
  • be remembered

Who it’s for

The business grew through referrals, reputation, relationships, or the owner’s own effort, and marketing was never built on purpose. It works, until it needs to work more reliably.

  • You have paying clients, and marketing has never been designed as a system.
  • You are doing the marketing yourself, or alongside someone who already wears several hats.
  • You want to know what matters, what to build first, and what can wait.

Marketing gets easier when the foundation is intentional.

What you’ll be able to do

  1. Get found

    Know where your best customers actually come from, and put your effort where it earns something.

  2. Get chosen

    Say what you do in language buyers recognize as theirs, so the decision gets easier to make.

  3. Be remembered

    Stay in mind between the first conversation and the decision, without it depending on one person.
