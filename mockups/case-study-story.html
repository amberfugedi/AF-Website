Same structure. The artifact takes the panel instead of the figure cards, so the pages differ because the work differs rather than because the template does.

Consulting & client work

A pressure washing company was doing good work under a brand that didn’t look like it.

The business

A2 Exteriors, a small local pressure washing company. Consultant.

What made it hard

The owner knew marketing mattered and had no idea where to start, which is the most common place a small business begins.

What I built

A brand from the ground up, carried onto what local service work actually runs on: yard signs, door hangers, flyers, the whole print kit.

What changed

One consistent look on every job site and doorstep in the service area, and a plan small enough to actually do.

A long case — the anatomy, then the chapters

The 600-word cases keep the same opening and anatomy, and their longer sections run underneath at full measure. One template, three lengths, and the reader always meets the same shape first.

Marketing leadership & growth

Marketing can hit every number it sets for itself and still not be trusted by the people carrying the revenue target.

Measured on sourced revenue not on lead volume, which rewards the cheapest lead
One pipeline, both functions so a disagreement about performance is about one set of figures

The context

Senior in-house marketing leadership. Written without naming the organization.

What made it hard

Marketing counted what it generated. Sales counted what it closed. Both sets of numbers were accurate and neither answered whether the money was turning into revenue.

What I owned

Demand generation end to end, the marketing investment behind it, and the enablement work between the two functions.

Working with Sales

This is the part that does the most work, and the part that is hardest to shortcut. It means agreeing what counts before the quarter starts rather than arguing about it afterwards, so a lead, an opportunity and a sourced deal mean the same thing on both sides of the house.

It means marketing looking at the same pipeline Sales looks at, in the same view. And it means marketing carrying part of the commercial number, not a separate one that can be hit in a quarter the business misses.
