Leading Marketing

I enjoy building teams, brands, and marketing functions that can grow with the business.

In-house · VP of Growth Marketing & Enablement · 2023–Present

PERQ

Challenge

A martech company in a high-growth phase that needed demand generation to keep pace, then a company-wide restructure that put the entire marketing function on the table.

Approach

Full ownership of marketing: brand and positioning, demand generation, website and content strategy, sales enablement, and marketing operations, with an $800K budget and the team leadership and cross-functional planning that come with it.

Impact

Inbound leads up 120% in five months, with the highest month in company history. Paid search cost per conversion cut in half while conversions rose 80%. Promoted twice in two years, most recently to VP of Growth Marketing & Enablement on the executive team.

In-house · Director of Marketing, 2021–2023 · Demand Generation

Scanoptics

Challenge

A decades-old document technology company repositioning toward intelligent automation, with a legacy marketing function and a pipeline that had not caught up with the new direction.

Approach

Rebuilt marketing from the foundation: defined the TAM and ICPs, implemented HubSpot CRM and automation, launched ABM, paid media, event, and outbound programs, and ran partner campaigns with Dropbox, UKG, and Xerox.

Impact

115% more opportunity creation, with pipeline growing from $1.3M to $2.8M, and 47% more inbound leads.

In-house · Industrial Website & Lead Capture
